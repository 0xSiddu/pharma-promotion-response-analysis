FINDINGS  [SIMULATED DEMO DATA]
Doctors analysed: 6,000 (55% received payments)
Overall: paid doctors' average prescriptions differ from unpaid doctors' by +5.3%.
Largest lift: Endocrinology (+15.6%).
After controlling for specialty and state (regression): going from $0 to $100 in
payments is associated with a +8.5% change in prescriptions, and $0 to $1,000
with a +12.9% change (coefficient 0.0176, p=1.02e-14).

CAVEAT: this is association, not causation. Companies may pay doctors who already
prescribe heavily. A matched-region pilot or before/after design would be needed
to estimate the true promotion effect.
